# TrustFolio 20-category premium 3D creative contract

This file locks the user-approved visual references uploaded in the current
conversation: (a) colorful cinematic Washing Machine story scenes with a
human problem hook, walking/talking animated appliances, real benefit cards
and a five-to-one ranking; and (b) the 20-category illustrated creative grid.

## Shared identity: all 20 categories

- Strong TrustFolio rich royal-purple, electric/royal blue, midnight navy and
  gold/yellow accents; white speech bubbles and gold rank-pill/CTA.
- Original category-specific movie environments with foreground, middle-ground
  and background depth, physically lit 3D characters, convincing contact
  shadows, cinematic camera moves and legible 9:16 framing.
- Same approved layout positions and visual hierarchy, but DIFFERENT
  storytelling, environment details, character personalities, props, sound,
  camera movement and problem/feature explanation for each category.
- 5 cheerful interacting product-character bodies with eyes, eyebrows, mouth,
  articulated hands and shoes, walking/reacting/talking. No flat card zooms.
- Genuine recognizable ranked models are ONLY presented after source snapshot,
  licensing, product-identity, price, brand and feature verification.
- The exact reference lady/avatar or official vector logo are NOT yet reusable
  licensed production assets. Concept wordmarks must not claim to be official.
- No claims of "live prices", "best due to X", performance tests, warranty, etc
  without a current source supporting that exact product's statement.

## Final ad story recipe

00-03 problem + expressive human or contextual hook.
03-06 five living product friends join the environment.
06-24 5 ranked characters play genuinely interactive scenes and explain each
product's actual useful features and suitability through source evidence.
24-28 source-verified 5-to-1 reveal (do not select your own winner).
28-30 TrustFolio CTA, official licensed logo and current price disclaimer.

Pure scripted dialogue such as "fifth said, fourth said" is prohibited. Every
line should answer a relatable need, react to another character, explain a
specific verified feature or make a funny natural observation.

## Hard production gate

Actual SKU photos/meshes, price-check provenance, authoritative rank, retailer
image reuse rights, official logo, natural human-reviewed Hindi voices, lip-sync
and creative QA are REQUIRED BEFORE an advertisement can publish.

The 20 Blender 3D short-video concepts here do NOT represent 20 completed
ranking advertisements. Each shows only a distinctive animated category
archetype and environment, not sourced retailer products or price claims.
The separate 6-second Washing Machine proof uses historical ranked photo
identities but remains private/not-for-publication.

20 original category scenes can be rendered and reviewed in the CI workflow
20-cinematic-worlds-demo.yml; pricing, product-ad publishing and existing
TrustFolio social image schedules remain untouched and disabled here.
